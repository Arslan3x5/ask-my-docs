# Northwind Home Goods

Customer and employee handbook. Fictional sample document for testing AskMyDocs.

## 1. Customer returns and refunds

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item is received.

Faulty items are replaced or refunded at no cost, and Northwind pays the return shipping.

For change-of-mind returns, a return shipping fee of 4.50 USD is deducted from the refund.

Gift cards are not refundable. Clearance items can be exchanged but not refunded.

## 2. Shipping and customer support

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 12 USD.

Orders above 75 USD ship free with standard delivery.

We ship to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom only.

Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern time are dispatched the same day.

Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern time, at support@northwind.example or 555-0142.

Chat replies arrive within 10 minutes during support hours, and email replies within 1 business day.

## 3. Employee leave and working arrangements

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year.

Leave requests longer than 5 days must be submitted at least 14 days in advance.

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

Parental leave is 16 weeks for the primary caregiver.

Remote work is allowed up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Core office hours are 10 AM to 4 PM.

## 4. Expenses and IT security

Employees may claim travel and meal expenses up to 60 USD per day while travelling.

Expense claims must be filed within 30 days and include receipts.

Any purchase above 500 USD needs written approval from the department head.

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and changed every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is required for all company accounts.

Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to the IT team within 24 hours at it-help@northwind.example.

Customer records are kept for 7 years and then securely deleted.
